# Lab 2 - Signal Acquisition and Data Analysis

This laboratory focuses on signal acquisition using LabVIEW-based instrumentation and data processing from measured waveforms.

## Objective

The purpose of the lab is to understand how acquisition systems capture signals, how to configure measurement parameters, and how to analyze the resulting data in a practical instrumentation environment.

## Main files

- `acquiring_a_signal.vi` – LabVIEW virtual instrument for signal acquisition
- `voltage.vi` – instrument or measurement routine related to the signal acquisition workflow
- `Run1.lvm` and `Run1.xlsx` – captured measurement data
- `backup.lvm` and `backup.xlsx` – backup data files
- `1Hz.xlsx`, `100Hz.xlsx`, `1K.xlsx`, `1M.xlsx` – measurements taken at different frequencies
- `EIO PRELAB2.pdf` and `LAB2_Instrument_driver_tutorial.pdf` – preparatory and tutorial documentation
- `EIO LAB2 - María Jover - Sergi Salvà.docx` and `lab2.docx` – lab reports
- `getting started labview.pdf` – introductory LabVIEW documentation

## Topics covered

The project includes work on:

- signal acquisition setup
- frequency-dependent measurements
- waveform observation and recording
- instrument control and data logging
- comparison of responses at multiple frequencies

## Learning outcomes

Students gain experience in:

- configuring acquisition systems
- interpreting signal behavior over time and frequency
- handling LabVIEW-based instruments
- analyzing and reporting measurement results

## Notes

This lab is centered on the practical side of electronic instrumentation, showing how data acquisition workflows are implemented and how measurement results are validated in real laboratory conditions.
