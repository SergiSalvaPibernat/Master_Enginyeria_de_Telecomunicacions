# Lab 4 - Rise Time and Signal Characterization

This project corresponds to the fourth EIO laboratory and is focused on the characterization of electronic signals, especially rise time and transient behavior.

## Objective

The goal of the lab is to study how fast-changing signals behave in real measurement systems and how rise time affects the interpretation of waveform data.

## Main files

- `eio_rise_time.pdf` – supporting material on rise time analysis
- `PRELAB 4 - María Jover Tarancón - Sergi Salvà Pibernat.docx` – preliminary preparation
- `LAB4 - María Jover Tarancón - Sergi Salvà Pibernat.docx` – final report for the laboratory

## Topics covered

This lab focuses on:

- rise time measurement
- transient response analysis
- bandwidth and signal fidelity considerations
- influence of instrumentation on measured waveforms
- practical evaluation of signal quality

## Learning outcomes

Students learn to:

- measure and interpret rise time in electronic signals
- evaluate the effect of measurement systems on signal accuracy
- connect theoretical concepts to practical oscilloscope measurements
- produce a technical report describing the observed behavior

## Notes

This lab is especially relevant for understanding how high-speed signals are measured and how instrument limitations can affect waveform interpretation in electronic engineering.
