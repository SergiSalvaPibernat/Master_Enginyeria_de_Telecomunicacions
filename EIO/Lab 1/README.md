# Lab 1 - Electronic Instrumentation and Measurements

This project corresponds to the first laboratory of the EIO subject and focuses on the fundamentals of electronic instrumentation and measurement techniques.

## Objective

The goal of the lab is to introduce students to the practical use of measurement equipment and the interpretation of signals, with emphasis on basic instrumentation concepts, data acquisition, and result analysis.

## Main files

- `1 - EIO LAB1.pdf` – the lab statement and theoretical support
- `EIO LAB1 Sergi Salvà Pibernat.docx` – the report written for the lab
- `Prelab 1 Sergi Salvà Pibernat.docx` – preliminary preparation before the practical work

## Topics covered

This first laboratory typically includes:

- signal measurement basics
- understanding instrument operation
- data capture and observation
- analysis of measured values
- comparison between expected and observed results

## Learning outcomes

By completing this lab, students develop skills in:

- instrument handling
- basic signal acquisition
- measurement interpretation
- technical reporting of experimental results

## Notes

This lab is the starting point of the EIO coursework and establishes the foundations for more advanced instrumentation and signal-analysis tasks in later labs.
